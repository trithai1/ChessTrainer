About

Welcome to my website built to help train your chess knowledge and bring you up to the next level. You will be given a mix of challenges that will test your ability to solve difficult puzzles while also mixing in normal game positions. Challenging your ability to recognize and differentiate critical positions from normal positions, so that you can learn to properly manage your time and get a feel for when a position requires careful analysis.
